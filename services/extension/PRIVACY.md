# Privacy Policy for Ephemera Browser Extension

**Last Updated:** January 15, 2026

## Introduction
Ephemera ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our browser extension collects, uses, and discloses your information.

## Data Collection

### Personal Information
We collect the following personal information when you log in:
- Email address (from your Ephemera account)
- Authentication tokens

### Usage Data
We collect anonymous usage data to improve our service:
- Number of inboxes created
- Extension error logs (optional)

### Web Content
The extension interacts with web pages you visit ONLY to:
- Detect email input fields (`input[type="email"]`)
- Inject the auto-fill interface
- Fill the email address you select

We **DO NOT** collect, store, or transmit the content of the websites you visit, your browsing history, or any other form data.

## How We Use Your Information
- **Account Management:** To authenticate you and sync your inboxes.
- **Functionality:** To provide the core features of creating and managing disposable emails.
- **Notifications:** To send you alerts when you receive new emails (if enabled).

## Data Storage
- **Local Storage:** Your authentication token and preferences are stored locally on your device using Chrome's secure storage API.
- **Transmission:** All data transmission to our servers (`api.manhquy.id.vn`) is encrypted via HTTPS.

## Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others.

## Your Rights
You can revoke the extension's permissions at any time via your browser settings. You can also delete your Ephemera account and all associated data via the web dashboard.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@manhquy.id.vn.
