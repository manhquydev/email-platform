Frequently Asked Questions

How long do emails persist? expand_more

On the free plan, emails are kept for 24 hours. Paid plans extend this up to 7 days or indefinitely if saved manually. Once deleted, they are cryptographically erased from our servers.

Can I use my own domain? expand_more

Yes, the Ghost and Spectre plans allow you to bring your own custom domain. You will need to add a few DNS records (MX, TXT) to verify ownership and route emails to us.

Is the API rate limited? expand_more

Yes, to prevent abuse. The Spectre plan offers significantly higher rate limits suitable for large-scale enterprise testing environments.
