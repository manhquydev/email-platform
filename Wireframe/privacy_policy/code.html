Ephemera is built on the core principle that privacy is a fundamental human right. Unlike traditional email providers that mine your data for advertising, Ephemera is designed to know as little about you as possible. This policy outlines exactly what we do—and more importantly, what we don't do—with your data.

dataset

1. Data Collection

We practice strict data minimization. We only collect the absolute minimum information necessary to provide our service.

  • check_circle Account Information: If you create a premium account, we store a hashed version of your password and your chosen username.
  • check_circle Payment Data: Payment processing is handled by third-party providers (Stripe/Crypto). We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.
  • check_circle Temporary Logs: Server logs are flushed every 24 hours and contain no personally identifiable information (PII).
settings_applications

2. Use of Information

The information we collect is used solely for the purpose of operating the Ephemera service. We do not use your data for profiling, advertising, or marketing.

Your disposable email addresses and the content within them are processed automatically to deliver messages to you. Once a disposable session expires or is manually deleted, all associated data is permanently erased from our active memory and databases.

lock

3. Zero-Knowledge Architecture

info Note: We cannot recover lost passwords because we never knew them in the first place.

Ephemera employs a zero-knowledge architecture for all stored emails. This means that all emails are encrypted on the client side before they ever reach our servers.

We utilize AES-256 encryption for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. The decryption keys are derived from your master password, which is never sent to our servers. Consequently, Ephemera staff, government agencies, or hackers cannot read your emails even if they gain access to our physical servers.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. This does not include trusted third parties who assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or servicing you, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

We may release your information when we believe release is appropriate to comply with the law, enforce our site policies, or protect ours or others' rights, property, or safety. However, due to our zero-knowledge architecture, the "information" we can release is extremely limited.

5. User Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy laws, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data.

Right to Erasure

You can delete your account at any time. This action is irreversible and wipes all associated data immediately.

Right to Portability

You can export your domain settings and configuration via our API or dashboard settings.
