# Ephemera Extension Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 20, 2026

## Overview

Ephemera is a browser extension that helps you create and manage temporary email addresses. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect

1. **Account Information**: Email address used for authentication
2. **Inbox Data**: Temporary email addresses you create and their messages
3. **Usage Analytics**: Anonymous usage patterns (features used, error rates)
4. **Extension Settings**: Your preferences (theme, notifications, auto-copy)

### What We Do NOT Collect

- Browsing history
- Personal files
- Passwords or credentials (except Ephemera login)
- Data from other websites (except email field detection when activated)
- Keystrokes or form inputs on other sites

## Data Storage

- **Local Storage**: Settings and cached inbox data stored locally in your browser
- **Cloud Storage**: Account data and messages stored on secure servers (api.manhquy.id.vn)
- **Encryption**: All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS

## Permissions Explained

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| `storage` | Save your settings and cached data locally |
| `alarms` | Schedule inbox expiration checks and notifications |
| `clipboardWrite` | Copy email addresses to clipboard when requested |
| `activeTab` | Detect email input fields on the current page (when activated) |
| `notifications` | Alert you when new messages arrive |
| `sidePanel` | Display extension in Chrome's side panel |
| `contextMenus` | Right-click menu to quickly create/paste emails |
| `scripting` | Inject email addresses into form fields (when requested) |

## Third-Party Services

- **Ephemera API** (api.manhquy.id.vn): Core service for email management
- No advertising networks
- No third-party analytics tracking services (only internal anonymous usage metrics)
- No data sold to third parties

## Data Retention

- **Temporary Inboxes**: Automatically deleted after expiration (configurable)
- **Permanent Inboxes**: Retained until you delete them
- **Account Data**: Retained until account deletion
- **Local Cache**: Cleared when you uninstall the extension

## Your Rights

You can:
- Export your data (via dashboard)
- Delete your account and all associated data
- Disable notifications and analytics
- Clear local storage at any time

## Security

- All API communications use HTTPS
- Content Security Policy (CSP) enforced
- No remote code execution
- Regular security audits

## Children's Privacy

Ephemera is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be communicated via extension update notes or in-app notification.
