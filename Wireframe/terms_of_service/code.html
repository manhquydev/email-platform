1. Introduction

By accessing or using the Services, you agree to be bound by these Terms. If you do not agree to these Terms, you may not access or use the Services.

Ephemera is designed for privacy-conscious developers and users who require temporary email solutions. We prioritize data minimalism and security above all else.

2. Account Security

You are responsible for safeguarding the password and API keys that you use to access the Services and for any activities or actions under your password or API keys. We encourage you to use "strong" passwords (passwords that use a combination of upper and lower case letters, numbers and symbols) with your account.

warning

API Key Security

Do not commit your API keys to public repositories (e.g., GitHub). Ephemera will automatically revoke keys found in public leaks to protect your account quota.

3. API Usage Limits

Ephemera provides various subscription tiers with specific rate limits to ensure quality of service for all users.

  • Free Tier: Limited to 100 requests per hour. No custom domains.
  • Developer Tier: 10,000 requests per hour. Custom webhook support.
  • Enterprise Tier: Unlimited requests (subject to fair use) and dedicated support.

Exceeding these limits may result in temporary throttling of your API access (HTTP 429). Persistent abuse may lead to account suspension.

4. Anonymity Guarantees

We are committed to the principle of data minimalism. Our architecture is designed to know as little about you as possible while still providing the service.

check_circle What We Store

  • Account email address (hashed)
  • Current API usage counters
cancel What We Do NOT Store
  • Your IP Address logs
  • Browser User-Agent strings
  • Payment Details (handled by Stripe)

5. Payment & Subscriptions

Paid services are billed on a subscription basis. You will be billed in advance on a recurring and periodic basis ("Billing Cycle"). Billing cycles are set either on a monthly or annual basis, depending on the type of subscription plan you select when purchasing a Subscription.

We use a third-party payment processor (Stripe) to bill you through a payment account linked to your account. The processing of payments will be subject to the terms, conditions, and privacy policies of the Payment Processor.

6. Termination

We may terminate or suspend your account immediately, without prior notice or liability, for any reason whatsoever, including without limitation if you breach the Terms. Upon termination, your right to use the Services will immediately cease.

If you wish to terminate your account, you may simply discontinue using the Services or delete your account via the user dashboard.
