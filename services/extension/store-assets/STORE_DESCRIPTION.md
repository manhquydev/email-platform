## Detailed Description

### Ephemera - Temporary Email Made Simple

**Protect your privacy with disposable email addresses.**

Ephemera lets you create temporary email addresses instantly, right from your browser. Perfect for:

✅ **Sign up for services** without revealing your real email
✅ **Avoid spam** from newsletters and promotional emails
✅ **Test applications** with multiple email addresses
✅ **Protect your identity** when downloading content or accessing gated resources

### Key Features

📧 **Instant Email Creation**
Create a new temporary email in one click. Random or custom addresses available.

⏱️ **Flexible Expiration**
Set emails to expire automatically or keep them permanently. Extend time with one tap.

🔔 **Real-time Notifications**
Get desktop alerts when new messages arrive. Never miss verification emails.

📋 **One-Click Copy & Auto-Fill**
Copy addresses instantly or auto-fill email fields on any website.

📱 **QR Code Sharing**
Generate QR codes to quickly transfer email addresses to mobile devices.

🎨 **Beautiful Dark Mode**
Easy on the eyes with automatic dark/light theme support.

♿ **Fully Accessible**
Keyboard navigable, screen reader compatible, WCAG 2.1 AA compliant.

### How It Works

1. Click the Ephemera icon in your toolbar
2. Create a new temporary email (random or custom)
3. Use it on any website
4. Receive messages directly in the extension
5. Let it expire or delete when done
