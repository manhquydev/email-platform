Acceptable Use Policy

v2.4 Last Updated: October 24, 2023

Ephemera is dedicated to providing a secure, private, and anonymous communication platform. To ensure the safety and reliability of our service for all users, we have established this Acceptable Use Policy (AUP). By using Ephemera, you agree to comply with the guidelines outlined below. Our goal is to protect user privacy without enabling malicious behavior.

block Prohibited Activities

Spam & Unsolicited Mail

mail_lock

Sending unsolicited bulk emails (spam) is strictly prohibited. You may not use our service to distribute advertisements, promotions, or solicitations to users who have not explicitly requested them.

Phishing & Deception

phishing

Creating misleading emails or domains to trick recipients into revealing sensitive information (passwords, financial data) constitutes a severe violation and will result in immediate termination.

Malware Distribution

bug_report

Using Ephemera to host, distribute, or link to malicious software, viruses, trojans, or worms is forbidden. Our scanners actively monitor for such signatures.

Illegal Activities

policy

Any use of the service for activities that violate local, state, national, or international laws is prohibited. We cooperate with law enforcement for valid legal requests despite our privacy focus.

code API Usage & Limits

api

Our developer APIs are designed to integrate disposable email functionality into your applications. To ensure quality of service for everyone:

  • check_circle Rate Limiting: Free tier keys are limited to 100 requests per minute. Enterprise keys have custom limits.
  • check_circle Scraping: Automated scraping of our web interface is prohibited. Please use the official API endpoints.
  • check_circle Key Sharing: API keys are for individual or organizational use and should not be publicly shared or sold.

warning System Abuse

You agree not to interfere with or disrupt the integrity or performance of the Ephemera Service or the data contained therein. This includes attempting to gain unauthorized access to the Service or its related systems or networks.

Load testing, penetration testing, or vulnerability scanning of our infrastructure without prior written permission is strictly prohibited and will be treated as an attack.

gavel Enforcement

Violations of this Policy may result in actions including but not limited to:

  • Temporary suspension of service
  • Permanent account termination
  • Blocking of IP addresses or domains
  • Removal of content
  • Reporting to relevant authorities

Our Discretion

Ephemera retains sole discretion to determine whether a violation of this Policy has occurred. We are not liable for any damages resulting from the enforcement of this policy.
